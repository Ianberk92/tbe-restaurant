F1 race week, Nov 19–21. Thursday night of the show is the first race night. Travel tips
  • Expect closures and heavy traffic on the Strip, Koval Ln, Sands Ave and Harmon Ave from early afternoon Thursday (in 2025, closures began about 1pm), plus lane work earlier in the week. First practice is around 5:30pm.
  • Paradise Rd and the Westgate are outside the race circuit. They're the easiest Thursday dinner spots.
  • From the LINQ, use the Monorail, which runs 24 hours Nov 17–23. For North Strip resorts, use the Vegas Loop. Skip cars and rideshare that day.
